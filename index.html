Blue Fern Ceramics

Handmade pottery, classes, and studio time on the north side of town.

Welcome to Blue Fern Ceramics. We are a small studio making wheel-thrown mugs, bowls, and planters, with glazes mixed in-house. We run beginner and intermediate classes, offer monthly memberships for experienced potters, and hold a seasonal market every December.
